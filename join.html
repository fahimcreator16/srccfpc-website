Do not leak any information of our club. If you violate any of these rules for the first time, you will be fined 500 Taka, and you will not be permitted to leave the club until this amount is paid. If you commit such a violation a second time, you will be expelled from the club, and an additional 500 Taka will be charged prior to your expulsion.
  4. Respect: Respect your seniors and be humble with your batchmates. Relationships among club members are strictly forbidden. No bullying and harassing. You will get all kinds of freedom but for that you must inform the superiors.

If you can't complete your task due to any undeniable reason, you have to inform any of your superior. Everyone has to use club instruments cautiously and if anyone loses any instrument, everyone has to take responsibility for it and have to pay for it.
  6. Academic activities: Everyone is responsible for their own academic results. The club will not take any responsibility for your academic failure. You have to learn to balance your extra curriculum activities side by side with your studies.
  7. Club rejoining: If you leave our club and then want to rejoin, you have to pay rejoining fees. [The rejoining fees are negotiable by the superiors]
  8. Development allowance: Club members are obliged to pay whatever expenses are required for the development of the club.
  9. Power structure: Executive member can give his opinion on any decision. Panel members will represent their departments and take leadership. Panel members may publish their decisions for club development with Mentor's permission. Executive have 1 star power. Mentor have 3 star power. Copyright holder have 4 star power. Moderator and Co moderator have 5 star power. 5 panel members can use 4 star power in case of emergency. Those members are PRESIDENT, CO, GS, VP, JS.
  10. Club materials: If any club-related materials or information are shared or taken outside the club without prior permission, a fine of BDT 500 will be imposed for the first offense. If the offense is repeated, the member will be banned from the club.
